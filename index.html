Tutorial

A step-by-step guide to getting started and making progress.

1. Start playing

Select Play to enter the fishing map. Your coin and fish counters appear in the top-left corner. Press Cast Line to start fishing.

When the fishing challenge appears, hold the mouse button or use Space, W, or the Up Arrow to keep the catch bar inside the moving green zone. Keep doing this until the progress bar is full.

2. Collect and manage your catches

Caught fish are added to your Backpack. Open the Backpack from the map or the menu to review your fish, bait, tanks, and other owned items.

Different fish have different values. You can sell eligible fish through the Player Market to earn coins.

3. Use bait and shop items

Buy items in the Shop with coins. If you own Worm Bait, equip it from your Backpack before casting a line to improve your fishing luck. You can unequip it from the same place.

4. Build your aquarium

Buy Mini Tanks or obtain other tank types, then open your Aquarium. Select an empty slot and choose a fish from your inventory to display it.

A fish displayed in a tank is no longer available in your loose fish inventory until you remove it. Removing a fish returns it to your inventory. You can also place compatible cosmetics on tanks.

5. Expand your aquarium

Purchase extra Aquarium Rooms in the Shop to unlock more space. Each room provides additional slots for displaying your collection.

6. Explore the community

Use Friends to search for players by their exact username, send requests, and visit friends' profiles and aquariums. You can also use the Player Market to browse offers from other players.

7. Follow achievements and updates

Open Achievements to see goals and rewards as you progress. Check News for game updates and Notifications for messages or account notices.

8. Visit Clippy's Workshop

Clippy's Workshop uses Pearls. Collect enough Pearls to unlock the workshop and exchange the required items for special tank rewards.

Game Rules

Please review community rules carefully.

1. Respect within the community
Offensive, toxic, or discriminatory content and language in profiles (pfp, username, bio) are strictly prohibited.
2. Zero cheating (Fair Play)
Exploits, external modifications, bots, or third-party tools used to alter fishing or market mechanics are not permitted.
3. Fair trading
Transactions in the player marketplace must be fair; the use of secondary accounts to inflate prices or artificially transfer currency is prohibited.
4. Account security
You are responsible for the security of your account. Do not share your login credentials with anyone. Administrators will never ask for your password.
5. Bug abuse & Cooldowns
Exploiting system lag, clicking rapidly to duplicate purchases or claim rewards improperly is strictly prohibited. All store and reward actions are protected with a mandatory 5-second cooldown.

Scroll to the bottom of the rules to unlock Accept button.
