Game Rules

Please review community rules carefully.

1. Respect within the community
The use of offensive, toxic, discriminatory topics or offensive language in profiles (pfp, username, bio) and chat is strictly prohibited.
2. Zero cheating (Fair Play)
Exploits, external modifications, bots, or third-party tools used to alter fishing or market mechanics are not permitted.
3. Fair trading
Transactions in the player marketplace must be fair; the use of secondary accounts to inflate prices or artificially transfer currency is prohibited.
4. Account security
You are responsible for the security of your account. Do not share your login credentials with anyone. Administrators will never ask for your password.
5. Bug abuse & Cooldowns
Exploiting system lag, clicking rapidly to duplicate purchases or claim rewards improperly is strictly prohibited. All store and reward actions are protected with a mandatory 5-second cooldown.
